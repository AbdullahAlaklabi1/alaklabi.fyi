cat about.txt

I work on practical multimedia security methods for protecting images and video. My research combines lightweight cryptographic design with chaotic systems, substitution–permutation networks, transform-domain processing, and data hiding.

My doctoral work studies how these components can be organized into efficient image and video protection pipelines, with emphasis on security evaluation, computational efficiency, and suitability for multimedia data.

I am a lecturer in Computer Science at Shaqra University and a doctoral researcher at Kansas State University.
