My name is Abdullah, and I am a Lecturer at Shaqra University and a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at Kansas State University. I am a member of the ISCAAS Lab, where my research focuses on multimedia security and applied cryptography.

My work focuses on efficient image and video encryption and lightweight steganography schemes for secure data hiding. I develop multimedia protection methods using substitution–permutation networks, chaotic logistic maps, SHA-256-based key generation, transform-domain processing, confusion–diffusion structures, and LSB substitution.

I earned an M.S. in Software Engineering from the University of St. Thomas in Minnesota and a B.S. in Software Engineering from the University of Hail in Saudi Arabia.
